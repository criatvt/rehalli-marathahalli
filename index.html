CIVIC COLLAPSE

COMMAND MANUAL

Your goal: complete 12 quarters while keeping the city functioning. Use Advance Quarter to bring the next decision to your desk. Read each option's costs and consequences before choosing.

  • SPACE — advance quarter. Use the visible buttons when navigating by keyboard or touch.
  • DRAG — orbit · SCROLL — zoom · M — toggle audio.
  • Lower is better: congestion, flood risk and AQI. Higher is better: ecology, economy, approval and water.
  • Collapse thresholds: congestion at 100%, ecology, economy or approval at 0%, or treasury below −₹40 CR. Costs may use emergency credit. Lake fires begin below 25% ecology; water below 20% erodes approval.
  • Protect your budget. Choices can seed future crises; open City Intelligence to inspect Pending Cascade and faction reactions.
  • Core Vitals remain at the bottom on small screens. Open Faction Standing for the political balance. The map is schematic, not a navigation aid.
